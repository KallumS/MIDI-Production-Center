## Goal

The owner asked for every app's window text to follow Apple's writing
guidelines, as Noterator and Miderator now do. This is that sweep for MIDI
Production Center ([ADR 0009](../adr/0009-window-text-follows-apples-writing-guidelines.md)).

## What was done

- **Panel** (`mpc_ui.jsfx-inc`): capitals became title style - "SAMPLE",
  "LOAD ITEM", "PANIC", "TRANSFER TO PADS", "BANK A", "PAD / FX / SETUP",
  "HOLD TO STUTTER", the FX names and the AUDIO / IN / OUT lights now read
  "Sample", "Load Item", "Panic", "Transfer to Pads", "Bank A", "Pad / FX /
  Setup", "Hold to Stutter", "Pitch Correction", "Audio / In / Out". Control
  labels went the other way, from sentence case to title style: "Trigger
  Note", "Play Mode", "Choke Group", "Output Channel", "Velocity Sensitivity"
  (was "Vel. sens"). "st" is "semitones"; "ON/OFF" and "on/off" are "On/Off";
  an empty pad shows "—"; the pad menu is "Copy Pad Settings", "Paste Pad
  Settings", "Reset Pad", "Play Whole Phrase", "Mute (No Slice)".
- **Messages** say what to do and name buttons as they read: "No phrase
  loaded — click Sample or Load Item", "This pad is empty — click Transfer to
  Pads", "Audio is off — see “No sound?” in the README". The header has about
  258 px, so each was rendered and kept to about forty characters - several
  of the old ones were already cut off there.
- **Slider names and enums** (REAPER's parameter list): "Comp Target/
  Threshold/Ratio/Makeup" are "Compressor …", "Pitch Correct" is "Pitch
  Correction", "1 bar, 2 bars" are "1 Bar, 2 Bars", "Low-pass" is "Low-Pass",
  "Sync to project" is "Sync to Project". **"Chop Mode" and "Instance ID" were
  left alone** - the loader scripts find the plug-in by those names.
- **Loader scripts**: their messages use curly apostrophes and quotes, an em
  dash, and say what to do ("There’s no MIDI Production Center in this
  project. Add “JS: MIDI Production Center” to a track, then try again.").
  REAPER's message box buttons cannot be relabelled, so they stay OK. File
  names - the action names - are unchanged.
- README quotes the new labels.
